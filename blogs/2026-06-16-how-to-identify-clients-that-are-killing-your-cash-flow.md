Some clients cost more to serve than they bring in, with late payments, scope creep, endless revisions, and unbilled admin tasks quietly draining cash flow even when revenue looks healthy. By tracking client behavior with simple tags and watching customer payment histories, businesses can spot which relationships are hurting their margins and how to fix them.
